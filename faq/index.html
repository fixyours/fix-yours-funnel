FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

The questions we hear most before someone books a strategy call. Can’t find yours here? Just ask.

Do you publish fixed prices or packages?

No. Every business is different, so pricing and scope are set together on a strategy call once we understand your business, goals and current setup — not pulled from a price list.

What’s the first step if I want to work with you?

Book a free strategy call. We’ll ask about your business, your goals and where leads or follow-up are currently falling short, then tell you honestly whether — and how — we can help.

Do you only work with solar and real estate businesses?

Those are two of our strongest niches, with the deepest experience, but the underlying system (lead generation, advertising, CRM, automation and content) applies to other local and service-based businesses too. Tell us about your business on a call.

What do you mean by “marketing automation & CRM”?

Automated client follow-up, lead nurturing and appointment booking — for example instant SMS or email replies to a new enquiry, reminders, missed-call text-back and review requests — all tracked in one connected pipeline instead of scattered across tools.

Will I be locked into a long contract?

We’ll walk through terms on your strategy call before anything is agreed. We’d rather earn an ongoing relationship by getting results than lock you in and hope you don’t notice.

Do you use AI in the work you deliver?

Yes, where it genuinely helps — AI-assisted video and content production, and automation for follow-up and lead nurturing. We use it where it saves real time or improves response speed, not for its own sake.

Do you have case studies or reviews I can see?

We’re actively building out both, and we won’t publish results, quotes or client names we haven’t actually delivered and had confirmed. Ask on a strategy call for a straight answer on what’s realistic for your industry.

How do I get in touch if I have a question first?

Email us at admin@fixyours.net, or book a strategy call directly — either way you’ll hear back from a real person.
